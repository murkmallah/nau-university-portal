About the Hostel

A Comfortable Home Within the Campus

The NAU Student Hostel offers on-campus residential accommodation for both male and female students in separate, fully secured blocks. Designed to support student well-being and academic focus, each block provides a structured, disciplined, yet comfortable living environment.

From furnished rooms and hygienic mess facilities to 24/7 security and high-speed Wi-Fi, our hostel ensures that resident students have everything they need to thrive during their academic journey at National Aviation University.

What We Offer

Hostel Facilities & Services

Everything a student needs for a safe, comfortable, and productive residential life on campus.

24/7 Security
Round-the-clock trained security guards, CCTV surveillance, and controlled entry/exit ensure the complete safety of all hostel residents at all times.
High-Speed Wi-Fi
Campus-wide high-speed internet connectivity is available throughout the hostel blocks, supporting online learning, research, and communication needs of students.
Mess & Dining
A hygienic and well-managed in-house mess facility provides nutritious breakfast, lunch, and dinner for hostel residents with a diverse weekly menu.
Power Backup
Uninterrupted power supply through generator backup ensures that fans, lighting, and essential appliances remain operational even during load shedding.
Clean Water Supply
Filtered drinking water and a reliable clean water supply for washrooms and bathrooms are maintained at all times within both hostel blocks.
Air Conditioning
Selected rooms and common areas in the hostel are equipped with air conditioning to provide a cool and comfortable environment especially during summer.
Accommodation

Room Types

Choose from a range of room configurations suited to different preferences and requirements.

Single Room

Private accommodation for one student

  • Single bed with mattress & pillow
  • Dedicated study table & chair
  • Personal wardrobe/cupboard
  • Attached or shared washroom
  • Air cooler / fan
  • High-speed Wi-Fi access

Double Sharing Room

Shared accommodation for two students

  • Two single beds with bedding
  • Two study tables & chairs
  • Individual wardrobes
  • Shared attached washroom
  • Ceiling fan & ventilation
  • High-speed Wi-Fi access

Triple Sharing Room

Shared accommodation for three students

  • Three single beds with bedding
  • Three study tables & chairs
  • Shared wardrobe space
  • Common washroom nearby
  • Ceiling fan & natural light
  • High-speed Wi-Fi access

Important Guidelines

Hostel Rules & Regulations

All residents are expected to follow these rules to ensure a safe, respectful, and disciplined hostel environment.

ID Card Mandatory

Every student must carry their university ID card at all times and show it at the gate when entering or leaving the hostel premises.

Curfew Compliance

All residents must return to the hostel before the designated curfew time. Late entry requires prior permission from the warden.

Maintain Silence

Noise and loud music are strictly prohibited during study hours and after lights-out. Residents must respect the peace of others.

No Smoking / Prohibited Items

Smoking, tobacco, alcohol, and any prohibited substances are strictly banned within all hostel premises. Violations lead to immediate action.

Keep Rooms Clean

Students are responsible for maintaining cleanliness in their rooms. Rooms are inspected periodically by hostel management staff.

Respect for All

Bullying, harassment, or disrespectful behavior towards any student, staff member, or visitor is strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action.
