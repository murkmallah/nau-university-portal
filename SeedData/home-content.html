Eligibility Criteria
Entry Requirements per Program — 2026
All candidates must meet the minimum academic qualifications listed below. Foreign nationals and CAA employees may be subject to additional requirements.
BE ANS Engineering / BE Airport Engineering
4 Years · 8 Semesters
FSc (Pre-Engineering) or A-Levels (Physics, Math, Chemistry/CS) with minimum 50% marks
NTS/AU Entry Test with qualifying score
Age limit: Not more than 22 years at time of admission
Pakistani National or approved foreign national
BS Aviation Management / BS Aviation Operations
4 Years · 8 Semesters
FA/FSc/ICS or equivalent with minimum 45% marks
NTS/AU Entry Test with qualifying score
Age limit: Not more than 23 years at time of admission
BS Aviation Cyber Security
4 Years · 8 Semesters
FSc (Pre-Engineering/ICS) or A-Levels with Math/CS with minimum 50% marks
NTS/AU Entry Test with qualifying score
Age limit: Not more than 22 years at time of admission
MBA Aviation Management
2 Years · 4 Semesters
Bachelor's degree (16 years of education) in any discipline with minimum 2nd Division (45%)
GAT General / NTS MBA Test with minimum qualifying score
Working professionals with aviation background preferred
Minimum 2 years post-graduation work experience (preferred)
Short courses are primarily designed for CAA/PAA employees, aviation industry professionals, and government nominees. Some courses are open to sponsored candidates from other organizations.
Aerodrome Fire Fighting Courses
1–5 Weeks
Matriculation (SSC) minimum qualification
Physically fit — medical clearance required
Nominated by employing organization (CAA/PAA/Airport Authority)
ATC / ANS / Management Courses
1–8 Weeks
Intermediate (FSc/FA) or equivalent for officer-level courses
Matriculation for staff-level courses
Official nomination letter from parent organization mandatory
Professional Courses
PCAA Type Ratings & ICAO-Endorsed Programs
PCAA Type Rating courses are fully endorsed and regulated by the Pakistan Civil Aviation Authority. Certificates are recognised for CAA licensing and regulatory compliance purposes.
ATR 42/72 Type Rating
6 Weeks PCAA Endorsed
Ground school and simulator sessions for ATR turboprop operations. Includes systems, limitations, and emergency procedures.
Boeing 737 Classic Type Rating
8 Weeks PCAA Endorsed
Comprehensive type rating covering B737-300/400/500 series. FFS sessions included at approved simulator facility.
Airbus A320 Family Familiarisation
10 Weeks PCAA Endorsed
Full A320/A319/A321 type rating course with fly-by-wire systems, FMGC operations, and CRM training.
ATC Procedures (ICAO Doc 4444)
4 Weeks PCAA Endorsed
Standardised ATC procedures per PANS-ATM. Radar vectoring, separation minima, and emergency handling.
ICAO-recognised courses follow TRAINAIR PLUS standards and are accepted internationally. These certificates are valid for regulatory and professional recognition across ICAO member states.
Aviation Safety Management (SMS)
1–2 Weeks ICAO Standard
Understanding and implementing Safety Management Systems per ICAO Annex 19 and Doc 9859.
Crew Resource Management (CRM)
1 Week ICAO Standard
Human factors, decision making, and crew coordination for flight and ATC operations teams.
Aviation Auditing Techniques
1 Week ICAO / USOAP
Regulatory audit methodology aligned with ICAO Universal Safety Oversight Audit Programme.
Aircraft Accident Investigation (Annex 13)
1 Week ICAO Standard
Accident and incident investigation procedures per ICAO Annex 13, evidence collection and reporting.
Aviation Security (Annex 17)
1 Week ICAO Standard
Aviation security standards, threat assessment, and countermeasures per ICAO Annex 17 and Doc 8973.
Aviation & Climate Change
1 Week ICAO Standard
CORSIA framework, carbon offsetting, sustainable aviation fuels and environmental impact management.
