History of CAA in Pakistan

Pakistan was created on 14th August 1947. In the same year, Pakistan ratified ICAO protocols and became a member of ICAO. In 1947, a Department of Civil Aviation within the Ministry of Defence (MoD) was established.

Pakistan Civil Aviation Authority (CAA) is a public sector autonomous body working under the Federal Government of Pakistan through the Aviation Division Cabinet Secretariat. CAA was established on 7th December 1982 through Pakistan Civil Aviation Authority Ordinance 1982. Before the creation of CAA, a Civil Aviation Department (CAD) in the Ministry of Defence (MoD) used to manage Civil Aviation related activities.

Ministry of Defence (MoD) continued to be the controlling Ministry even after the creation of CAA on 7th December 1982. However, in June 2013, the Government of Pakistan assigned this responsibility to the Cabinet Secretariat (Aviation Division), which was later transformed into the Ministry of Aviation in Aviation Division.

The primary purpose of establishing CAA is to provide for the promotion and regulations of Civil Aviation activities and to develop an infrastructure for safe, efficient, adequate, economical and properly coordinated Civil Air Transport Service in Pakistan.

The day of 7th December carries special significance — it coincides with the date of creation of the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) in 1944 as a result of the famous Chicago Convention. The United Nations has declared 7th December as the International Civil Aviation Day, celebrated every year all over the world. The creation of CAA on 7th December reflects the importance of Pakistan as a state to ICAO.

The restructuring process undertaken by CAA between 2006 and 2010 enabled the Organization to meet the requirements of ICAO. During the Universal Safety Oversight Audit Program (USOAP), ICAO audited Pakistan in June 2011 and rated Pakistan at approximately 83% compliant against the world average of 65%.

Since 2016, efforts have been underway to further improve the authority's functioning, ultimately resulting in the bifurcation of PCAA into three entities: Pakistan Civil Aviation Authority (PCAA) as the aviation regulator, Pakistan Airports Authority (PAA) for Air Navigation and Airport Services, and the Bureau of Air Safety Investigation (BASI) as an independent accident investigation body. Primary legislation for these three entities was promulgated in 2023.

Key Milestones

1944
Chicago Convention — ICAO founded on 7th December
1947
Pakistan ratifies ICAO protocols; Dept. of Civil Aviation established
1982
CAA formally established on 7th December via Ordinance 1982
2006
CAA restructuring process begins to align with ICAO standards
2011
ICAO USOAP audit — Pakistan rated 83% compliant (vs 65% world avg)
2013
Control transferred to Cabinet Secretariat (Aviation Division)
2023
Primary legislation for PCAA, PAA and BASI promulgated

International Civil Aviation Day

The United Nations declared 7th December as International Civil Aviation Day — the same date CAA was established, symbolising Pakistan's commitment to global aviation standards.
