About the Cafeteria

Wholesome Dining at the Heart of Campus

The NAU Cafeteria & Dining facility is designed to be more than just a place to eat — it's a social hub where students, faculty, and staff come together. Offering freshly prepared, hygienic, and affordable meals throughout the day, our cafeteria serves a variety of Pakistani and international dishes to cater to all tastes.

With a spacious, air-conditioned dining hall, outdoor seating areas, and a welcoming atmosphere, the cafeteria provides the perfect environment to relax, recharge, and connect with fellow students.

Dining Features

Why Students Love Our Cafeteria

Hygiene & Quality
Meals are prepared in a fully sanitized kitchen following strict food hygiene protocols. Ingredients are sourced fresh daily from trusted vendors.
Affordable Pricing
Student-friendly meal prices with special subsidized rates available to ensure that every student has access to nutritious, hot meals throughout the day.
Air-Conditioned Hall
A large, fully air-conditioned dining hall providing a comfortable and pleasant environment for meals, group discussions, and relaxation.
Vegetarian Options
A dedicated selection of vegetarian and dietary-friendly meals available daily so that all students can find something suitable for their preferences.
Tea & Café Corner
A dedicated tea and café corner serving hot and cold beverages, snacks, and light refreshments throughout the day — perfect for a quick break.
Special Event Catering
The cafeteria team provides catering support for university events, seminars, workshops, and official functions held on campus.

Important Information

Dining Guidelines

Student ID Required

Present your valid NAU student or staff ID card to avail subsidized meal rates at the cafeteria counter.

No Smoking Zone

The entire cafeteria and dining premises are strictly non-smoking to maintain a healthy environment for all users.

Keep it Clean

All students and staff are requested to dispose of waste responsibly and return trays to the designated collection areas.
