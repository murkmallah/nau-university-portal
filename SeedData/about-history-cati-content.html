Vision 2030

Transforming CATI into a world-leading aviation institution — advancing technology, curriculum, and industry partnerships.

The Civil Aviation Training Institute (CATI) has been an integral part of Pakistan's aviation landscape, evolving alongside the Pakistan Civil Aviation Authority to meet the growing demand for skilled aviation professionals in the country and the region.

Established to provide specialized aviation training in support of PCAA's mandate, CATI has continuously expanded its scope — from foundational aviation courses to advanced degree programs covering engineering, operations, IT, and management sciences.

With the restructuring of Pakistan's civil aviation sector and the formation of the National Aviation University (NAU), CATI has transformed into a comprehensive academic institution offering undergraduate and postgraduate programs that are internationally aligned with ICAO training standards.

We are advancing towards our CATI Vision 2030, working diligently to achieve our goals and transform CATI into a world-leading aviation institute. For that, we seek your continued support, sincere prayers, and valuable feedback.

Located at Airport Road, Hyderabad, Sindh, NAU provides students with a unique advantage — proximity to an active aerodrome — enabling direct exposure to real-world aviation environments throughout their academic journey.

Today, NAU stands as a symbol of Pakistan's commitment to developing indigenous aviation talent and reducing reliance on foreign expertise, with programs spanning Air Traffic Management, Airport Engineering, Avionics, Cyber Security, and Aviation Management.
