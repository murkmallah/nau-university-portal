Scholarships

Financial Aid & Scholarships

NAU offers merit and need-based scholarship programs to ensure that financial constraints do not prevent talented students from pursuing an aviation career.

Scholarship applications must be submitted within the first three weeks of each semester. Late applications will not be entertained. Maintaining the scholarship requires fulfilling academic and conduct requirements each semester.
Merit Scholarship
Top academic performers
50% – 100%
Fee waiver on tuition
  • Minimum 85% marks in the preceding qualifying exam (FSc/Intermediate)
  • Top 3 positions in AU Entry Test within respective program
  • Continuation requires CGPA of 3.5 or above each semester
  • No disciplinary action on record

Payment Methods

How to Pay Your Fees

NAU accepts fee payments through multiple channels for your convenience. Always keep your payment receipt safe — it is your official proof of payment.

Bank Challan
Recommended method
1
Log in to Student Portal and generate your fee challan
2
Print the challan or take a screenshot of the challan number
3
Visit any HBL, NBP, MCB, or UBL branch and pay over the counter
4
Collect the stamped challan receipt and submit a copy to the Accounts Office
Online & Mobile Banking
Pay from anywhere
1
Generate your challan from the Student Portal
2
Open your bank's app or internet banking (HBL Mobile, Easypaisa, JazzCash, UBL Digital, etc.)
3
Select Utility / Institution Payments → Search for NAU / CATI Hyderabad
4
Enter your challan number, verify the amount, and confirm payment
5
Save the digital transaction receipt (PDF/screenshot) and upload to the portal
