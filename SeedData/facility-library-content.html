About the Library

A Hub of Knowledge at the Heart of NAU

The NAU Library & Resource Center is a comprehensive academic library designed to support the research, learning, and professional development needs of students, faculty, and staff. Housing a rich collection of aviation, engineering, management, and regulatory resources — both in print and digital format — our library provides the tools you need to excel.

From quiet individual study areas to collaborative group rooms, the library offers a modern, welcoming environment where knowledge meets innovation.

What We Offer

Library Services

Comprehensive resources and services designed to support every stage of your academic journey.

Print Collection
Access thousands of textbooks, reference volumes, journals, and periodicals covering aviation, engineering, management, and regulatory sciences.
Digital Resources
Full access to international e-databases, ICAO publications, CAA regulations, e-books, and academic journals via our online portal.
Group Study Rooms
Bookable group study rooms equipped with whiteboards, projectors, and high-speed internet for collaborative academic projects.
Research Assistance
Experienced librarians available to guide students and faculty in conducting in-depth literature reviews and research activities.
Computer Lab
Over 40 fully equipped workstations with internet access, Microsoft Office, and aviation-specific software available to all registered students.
Printing & Scanning
Affordable printing, photocopying, and document scanning services available for students and faculty throughout the semester.
