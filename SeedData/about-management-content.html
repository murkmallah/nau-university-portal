Section 03

Management & Governance

The general direction and administration of CAA and its affairs vest in the CAA Board, which exercises all powers, performs all functions, and does all acts and things that need to be exercised, performed, or done by the Authority. The Chairman CAA Board is the Secretary of the Division to which the affairs of the Authority are allocated — presently the Secretary Aviation.

The CAA Executive Committee is the highest decision-making body of the Organisation, exercising administrative, executive, financial, and technical powers as delegated by the Authority. The Director General CAA chairs the Executive Committee and is appointed by the Federal Government as the Executive head of CAA.

Core Functions

Regulatory

Overseeing all civil aviation regulations, licensing, and compliance across Pakistan's airspace.

Air Navigation Services

Managing en-route and terminal air traffic services to ensure safe and efficient airspace utilisation.

Airport Services

Operating and developing airport infrastructure to support safe, efficient civil air transport.

OFFICIAL INSTITUTIONAL POLICY

PAA Integrated Management System (IMS) Policy

PAA Standards & Principles

Pakistan Airports Authority (PAA) is committed to be a world-class service provider in the aviation industry by providing safe, secure and efficient best-in-class aviation services to the stakeholders. The core values i.e. integrity, professional excellence, commitment, safety & security and customer focus, shall serve as the fundamental principles that guide PAA's actions and decisions, shaping its culture and overall functioning.

PAA commits to develop, implement, maintain and continually improve an effective and proactive Integrated Management System (IMS) in accordance with the requirements of national / international standards contained in ICAO annexes, Aviation Safety Management System, Aviation Security Management System, Quality Management System, Occupational Health & Safety Management System, Environmental Management System, Information Security Management System, Energy Management System and Customer Satisfaction for enhancing operational effectiveness.

PAA also commits to:

Comply with applicable legislation and regulatory requirements concerning its activities and services.
Continually improve safety performance, security measures, quality of its activities/ services and prevent environmental pollution.
Prevent occupational injury and ill-health by managing safety risks at workplace to promoting a culture in which all its employees work in a safe and healthy environment.
Evolve a positive safety and quality culture in all activities & services which fosters safe practices, encourages non-punitive safety reporting by recognizing that safety is paramount at all times.
Provide services of highest possible level to meet customer satisfaction.
Implement energy conservation program including use of renewable / alternate energy resources.
Implement National Civil Aviation Security Program and Quality Control Program to safeguard against unlawful interference to ensure satisfactory security measures in compliance with national and international obligations for reducing security risks in aviation business.
Provide framework and resources for hazards' identification, reporting and risk management.
Provide mechanism for setting up and review of IMS objectives, targets and performance indicators.
Adopt zero tolerance towards violation of procedures and standards pertaining to its operations.
Adopt principles of protection of safety data, safety information and related sources / softwares to ensure their continued availability for purposes other than maintaining or improving safety in accordance with national laws, regulations and policies.
Take effective measures to continually improve its systems through providing training to its employees on Integrated Management System to enable them in carrying out their jobs.
Accountability & Delivery All management levels and employees of PAA are accountable to meet their obligations towards Integrated Management System while performing their tasks for the delivery of the highest level of performance.

Governance Bodies

CAA Board

The supreme governing body responsible for overall direction, policy, and administration. Chaired by the Secretary Aviation.

CAA Executive Committee

Highest decision-making body for day-to-day affairs, chaired by the Director General CAA, exercising delegated powers.

Director General CAA

The Federal Government-appointed Executive Head of CAA, responsible for all executive functions and powers.

CAA HR Committee

Assists the CAA Board on all matters pertaining to human resources, appointments, and personnel policies.

CAA Audit Committee

Provides independent oversight of financial controls, audit findings, and accountability mechanisms.

CAA Quality Council

Headed by DG CAA, sets macro-level objectives and oversees adherence to vision, mission, and International Management System Standards.

Post-2016 Restructuring

Bifurcation of PCAA into three independent entities — legislation promulgated in 2023.

PCAA

Pakistan Civil Aviation Authority — serves as the independent aviation regulator of the country.

PAA

Pakistan Airports Authority — provides Air Navigation Services and Airport Services across Pakistan.

BASI

Bureau of Air Safety Investigation — independent body for aircraft accident & incident investigations.
